# Artwork credits and meme attributions

The 50 cat collectibles in `web/cats/` feature iconic, globally recognized cat memes and viral reaction cats. All images are bundled locally with the add-on; the add-on never downloads artwork or sends study data over the network at runtime.

Aspect-preserving rendering (`object-fit: contain`) is used throughout the add-on to preserve the full original composition, expressions, and captions of every asset.
